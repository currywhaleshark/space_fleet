# Third-party code

## Carbon Trinity (MIT)

Space Fleet includes modified C# adaptations of effect scheduling and debris scaling from
[Carbon Trinity](https://github.com/carbonengine/trinity), revision
`c4fd6af4fcda38416cdd6a044a04740f6e06aad6`.

Copyright (c) 2026 Fenris Creations. Original source headers additionally credit CCP ehf.

The full MIT license is in [third_party/carbon-trinity/LICENSE.md](third_party/carbon-trinity/LICENSE.md).
Preserve that copyright and permission notice with distributions containing the adapted code.
Original source snapshots, hashes and a function-by-function adaptation map are in
[third_party/carbon-trinity](third_party/carbon-trinity/README.md).

The port does not include EVE game assets or proprietary SDKs. Space Fleet's Godot shaders,
surface projection, geometry breakup and generated media are separate from the Carbon source.
